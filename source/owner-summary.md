# Website review for 101evler

Emin Khanlarov · 9 November 2026

I used www.101evler.com the way an ordinary visitor would: on a computer, on a simulated slow mobile connection, and with the keyboard only. These are the points that would help your visitors most.

## What is working well

- **The site is secure and its links are tidy.** Every visit is encrypted, and page addresses are short and readable, so they are safe and easy to share.
- **Dead ends are handled kindly.** When a page or listing no longer exists, visitors get a clear message and links to carry on, not a broken page.

## The three things to fix first

### 1. Forms, links and buttons have no names

**What visitors experience:** The boxes in the "send a message" form show their hint text only until you start typing. Many photo links and icon buttons have no name at all, so people who use a screen reader (software that reads the page aloud) hear only "link" or "button".
**Why it matters to you:** The contact form is how buyers reach your agents. Blind and partially sighted visitors cannot use it on their own.
**What to ask your web developer:** "Please add a visible label to every form field, a short description to every photo and logo, and a spoken name to every icon button."

### 2. Visitors cannot zoom in on phones

**What visitors experience:** On some phones, pinching to enlarge small text such as prices does nothing.
**Why it matters to you:** Older visitors and people with weak eyesight may give up on a listing they cannot read.
**What to ask your web developer:** "Please remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport setting so that zooming works."

### 3. A blank screen on slow connections

**What visitors experience:** On a slow mobile connection, the page stays white for about two and a half seconds, and about a third of what it downloads is advertising and tracking code, not homes.
**Why it matters to you:** Some visitors leave before anything appears, and every visit uses more of their mobile data than needed.
**What to ask your web developer:** "Please load the advertising and statistics scripts after the listings appear, show something while the page loads, and let browsers keep photos for longer."

## How to check it is fixed

1. In Google Chrome, run the free "Lighthouse" check on a listing page: the Accessibility score should rise from 53 to above 90, and clicking the word above a form field should put the cursor in that field.
2. Open a listing on your phone and pinch to zoom in on the price: the text should get bigger.
3. Open the home page on your phone using mobile data, not Wi-Fi: something useful should appear at once instead of a white screen.

Thank you for running a site that so many people rely on to find a home in North Cyprus; I hope these points are useful.
